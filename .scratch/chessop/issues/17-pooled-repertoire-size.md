# Pooled repertoire: how big is the position graph ADR 0006 builds, and does pooling admit junk?

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 
Map: [Chess opening trainer by sampling](../map.md)

## Question

Ticket 11 (ADR 0006, `docs/adr/0006-position-graph.md`) made the repertoire a DAG of positions keyed by EPD with game counts pooled over move orders, built by `build-snapshot` and cut by ADR 0005's rule (main moves = top-3 edges plus any edge with >= 5 % of the pooled games reaching the position, each clearing a floor of 0.02 % of the band's games; then prune every position with no exactly-named book position at or below it; then keep what the start position reaches). The decision was made on structure; this ticket supplies the numbers the spec will quote and checks the one risk the learner accepted blind. Measure, on the same sample as `docs/research/repertoire-rule-candidates-recent.md` (the August 2026 dump streamed until band 1800-2100 has 150,000 games, ADR 0003's filters, band 1800-2100 and, if cheap, 1500-1800):

- **Size of the pooled repertoire** against the sequence-tree one ADR 0005 measured (389 lines / 996 positions / 17 plies at 1800-2100): positions, edges, root-to-leaf paths (branches), leaf positions, maximum depth, book lines covered (of the 996 the band's traffic reaches at the floor), and how many of the 195 transposition-only lines come back.
- **Cycles**: how many (position, move) edges `build-snapshot` would drop to keep the graph acyclic, and what they are (a knight shuffle, a repetition); ADR 0006 keeps the edge on the shorter path.
- **Junk admitted by pooling**: positions or edges that clear the floor only as a sum of several orders each far below it (report the distribution of "largest single order's share of the pooled count" over kept positions, and list the kept positions where no single order holds >= 25 %), and whether any such position is a main move nowhere the learner would meet it by a main order.
- **Names**: share of kept positions with an exact book name; positions with named ancestors under more than one book name (the inherited-name ambiguity ADR 0006 avoids by storing exact names only); the canonical (most popular) move order per named position against the book's own line, how often they differ.
- **Per-colour split**: kept White-to-move and Black-to-move learner positions, since the record key is now the EPD and the root's per-colour need sums over each.

Write findings to `docs/research/pooled-repertoire.md` with the exact commands. Extend `refs/lichess/repertoire-rule-analysis-recent.py` (it already pools per EPD for the transposition-only count) or add `refs/lichess/pooled-repertoire-analysis.py` importing from it; stream the dump as that script does, write nothing large to disk. Numbers only, no recommendation.

## Answer

Measured on ticket 13's sample (the 2026-08 dump streamed until band 1800-2100 had 150,000 games, ADR 0003's filters; 1500-1800 with 214,825 games alongside), ADR 0005's rule on the pooled position graph of ADR 0006 (floor 0.02 % = 30 games at 1800-2100, top-3 plus 5 % share of the pooled count, book-pruned). Full tables, definitions and commands in [docs/research/pooled-repertoire.md](../../../docs/research/pooled-repertoire.md), script `refs/lichess/pooled-repertoire-analysis.py`. Numbers below are band 1800-2100 unless stated.

- **Size**: **1,104 positions / 1,326 edges / 1,985 branches (root-to-leaf paths) / 310 leaf positions / max depth 17 plies**, 794 internal positions with 1.67 kept moves on average (max 7), discard 16.7 %. The sequence tree ADR 0005 measured is 996 nodes but only **772 distinct positions** / 389 lines; merged by position it is 772 / 881 edges / 789 paths. The pooled graph holds every one of those 772 (**0 lost**) plus **332 recovered positions** (127 exactly named). Before book pruning: 3,074 positions / 6,018 paths / depth 21. Depth profile peaks at plies 6-7 (171, 161); nothing hits the ply cap. 783 stub edges (62,699 games). At 1500-1800: 780 / 960 / 1,070 / 241 / depth 15 (sequence tree 729 nodes = 561 positions / 316 lines); 4 sequence-tree positions are lost there because pooling enlarges the share base (French Tarrasch `3.Nd2` at 223 games drops below 5 % of the pooled `1.e4 e6 2.d4 d5` position; Italian Rousseau Gambit `3...f5`).
- **Book lines covered**: **662 of the 996** named positions with >= 30 pooled games (sequence tree 535). Of the 195 transposition-only lines **108 come back**, 87 stay out; of the 266 width-only misses 19 come in. The 334 still missed: 137 have an in-edge clearing the floor that is main at no parent (width: `1.Nf3` at 5,499 games is rank 4 and 3.7 % at the root, `1.e4 d6`, `1.e4 g6`), 132 hang under such a position, 65 have no single in-edge with 30 games. At 1500-1800: 505 of 793, 71 of 135 transposition-only recovered.
- **Cycles**: **0 edges dropped** in either band (no kept edge arrives at a position already reached at a shorter depth, so no repetition and no longer-order arrival). 15 kept positions are reached at two plies somewhere in the 3.7 M-position trie, but never by a main move.
- **Junk admitted by pooling**: over the 1,104 kept positions the largest single order holds 100 % for 95, 75-100 % for 482, 50-75 % for 252, 25-50 % for 189 and **below 25 % for 86**; 163 positions and 233 of the 1,326 edges have no single order clearing the floor. 200 kept positions are reachable by no single main order (absent from the sequence tree before pruning); **234 edges are main under no single move order** and 165 positions are entered only by such edges. Of the 86 positions below 25 %, **70 are main moves the learner would meet by no main order** (all their kept in-edges are pooled-only); the other 16 are systems reached by many popular orders (London with ...e6: 476 games over 17 orders, largest 22 %; Colle: 419 over 24). Every one is listed in the doc. At 1500-1800: 44 below 25 %, 32 of them pooled-only.
- **Names**: **662 of 1,104 kept positions (60.0 %) carry an exact book name**; **159 unnamed positions inherit more than one name** along their kept paths (widest 10; e.g. `1.d4 e6 2.c4` is under Agincourt Defense and Horwitz Defense; `1.d4 Nf6 2.c4 e6` under three); the canonical (most popular) order differs from the book's own line for **107 of 662** named positions (French Exchange via `2.Nf3`, Caro-Kann Main Line via `3.Nc3`, QGD Three Knights, Chigorin `1.d4 d5 2.Nc3 Nf6`), and the book's own order has no game for 10 and fewer than the floor for another 121. At 1500-1800: 64.7 % named, 102 ambiguous, 78 of 505 differ.
- **Per-colour split**: **546 White-to-move / 558 Black-to-move** kept positions (internal 398 / 396, leaves 148 / 162, named 331 / 331, recovered 167 / 165); 1500-1800: 389 / 391.
