# 30: Adopt by merging

**What to build:** A learner who signs in on a second device that already holds anonymous history keeps both histories. A position on one side only carries over; on both, the record with the later last pass wins whole. Both round logs are kept. A daily-score day on both sides keeps the account's Score and adds the anonymous rounds and successes; a day on one side only carries over. The account's settings win if it has any. The anonymous learner is then deleted. (Spec §5, glossary: Adopt.)
