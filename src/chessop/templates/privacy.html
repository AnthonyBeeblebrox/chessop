Privacy

This page says what chessop keeps about you, why, who else sees it and for how long. chessop keeps only what the drill you came for needs, so it never has to ask for your consent, and there is no cookie banner.

Who is in charge of your data

chessop is run by {{ controller }}, who decides what happens to your data (the "controller", in the words of the GDPR, the EU's data protection law). Write to {{ mail }} about anything on this page.

What chessop keeps, and why

You can play without giving anything: an email address or a Lichess account is needed only to sign in. No decision about you is ever made by a machine.

Tips on Ko-fi

If you tip chessop on Ko-fi, Ko-fi takes the payment and handles your details as its own controller, under its own privacy policy. No donor data reaches chessop.

Who receives data

  • OVH hosts the server, in France.
  • Scaleway sends the sign-in emails and keeps the encrypted backups, in Paris.
  • Lichess, only when you choose to sign in with it.

All of them are in France: no data goes outside the EU. GoatCounter, the statistics tool, runs on chessop's own server. UptimeRobot checks that the site is up and sees no personal data.

The cookie

chessop sets one cookie, named chessop. It holds a random key that points to your history or your sign-in, lasts 12 months from your last visit, and is used for nothing else: never for statistics or advertising. It is set only once you play a round, save your settings, opt an opening in or out, or sign in. The audience statistics set no cookie; unticking the box above stores skipgc in this browser so that it is not counted.

How long things are kept

  • History without an account: 12 months after your last visit; 30 days if you played fewer than 5 rounds.
  • Accounts: 24 months after your last visit. Email accounts get one warning at 23 months and are deleted only if you still do not visit within 30 days; Lichess accounts get no warning.
  • Error logs: 6 months at most. There are no visit logs.
  • Audience statistics: 760 days. The daily usage totals, which hold nothing about anyone: for good.
  • Backups: 14 days on the server, 30 days in the encrypted copy kept elsewhere, and the copies taken before each of the last 3 updates of the site.

Your rights

  • See and export (access and portability): download everything chessop keeps about you on Your data.
  • Correct it: change your email address on your account page.
  • Erase it: "Forget this browser's history" on Your data, or delete your account; either is done at once. Deleted data leaves the backups within 30 days.
  • Restrict or object to what chessop does with it: write to {{ mail }}.

For any of these, or anything else, write to {{ mail }}: you get a reply within one month. You can also leave instructions about what should happen to your data after your death (French law): write them to the same address. If you think chessop treats your data wrongly, you can complain to the CNIL, France's data protection authority: cnil.fr.
